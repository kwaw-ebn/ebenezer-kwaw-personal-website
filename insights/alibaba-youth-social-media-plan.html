Alibaba Youth Social Media Plan

By Ebenezer Kwaw · 25 September 2026 · Independent strategy case study
View original report ↗View portfolio ↗

An independent social-media planning exercise focused on younger, mobile-first and value-conscious e-commerce audiences.

Project typeIndependent strategy case study
DisciplineSocial Media Strategy
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

Large product catalogues and deal-led positioning can make it difficult to maintain relevance, trust and consistent engagement with younger shoppers.

The strategy

Use platform-specific short video, creator partnerships, interactive content and time-sensitive offers to connect affordability with entertainment.

My contribution and execution

  • Reviewed brand position, audience behavior and the competitive context.
  • Defined Instagram, X and YouTube roles.
  • Proposed short videos, influencer partnerships, polls, challenges and flash-sale content.
  • Created example content themes, KPIs, a calendar and budget framework.

Tools and methods

Audience analysis, SWOT, social-channel planning, content calendar and KPI design

Outcome, evidence and limitations

The project is an academic or portfolio strategy exercise. Its reach, click and conversion figures are proposed targets rather than verified campaign outcomes.
