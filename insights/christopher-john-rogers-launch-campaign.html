Christopher John Rogers Collection Launch Campaign

By Ebenezer Kwaw · 25 September 2026 · Independent campaign plan
View original report ↗View portfolio ↗

An independent integrated campaign plan connecting social media, paid advertising, email acquisition and e-commerce conversion for a fashion collection launch.

Project typeIndependent campaign plan
DisciplineIntegrated Marketing Campaign
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

A collection launch needs coordinated awareness, audience growth, email capture and purchase pathways rather than disconnected promotional posts.

The strategy

Build phased awareness, engagement and conversion across Instagram, Facebook, email and the website, supported by audience segmentation and retargeting.

My contribution and execution

  • Defined campaign objectives and a measurement framework.
  • Planned influencer content, teaser reels, behind-the-scenes content, carousel ads and retargeting.
  • Designed list-building routes through social profiles, website forms and lead ads.
  • Structured email flows for existing customers and new prospects, including message-testing concepts.

Tools and methods

Meta campaign planning, Mailchimp, GA4 measurement design, segmentation and A/B testing

Outcome, evidence and limitations

This portfolio project presents campaign targets and a testing approach. Except where clearly described as an illustrative A/B insight, the KPIs are planning targets and not verified commercial outcomes.
