A portfolio strategy demonstrating how competitor intelligence and content systems can support organic acquisition for an African fintech brand.

DisciplineSEO and Content Strategy
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

The case identified visibility, authority, acquisition and structural gaps, including weak rankings for high-intent searches, limited content coverage and fragmented internal linking.

The strategy

Use competitor intelligence and keyword gaps to build an intent-led content engine connected to product and conversion pages.

My contribution and execution

  • Conducted competitor and keyword-gap analysis.
  • Defined visibility, intent and conversion as the north-star measurement areas.
  • Designed technical and on-page improvements covering headings, metadata, URLs, sitemaps, site speed, alt text and internal linking.
  • Created a 90-day roadmap covering foundation work, content production, schema and authority building.

Tools and methods

Ubersuggest, competitor analysis, keyword research, technical SEO, content clusters and internal linking

Outcome, evidence and limitations

The project produced a scalable organic-growth roadmap and identified high-value search opportunities. It is a strategic portfolio case study and does not claim access to M-KOPA’s internal implementation or performance data.
