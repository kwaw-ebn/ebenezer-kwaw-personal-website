A digital strategy for a Ghanaian interlocking-brick company combining website improvement, SEO, AI search visibility, authority content and lead generation.

DisciplineSEO, AEO and Lead Generation
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

The audit found low social visibility, inconsistent posting, limited proof, weak calls to action, missing WhatsApp integration and an underused leadership voice.

The strategy

Connect Facebook awareness, LinkedIn authority and a search-optimized website to a clear enquiry and site-visit funnel.

My contribution and execution

  • Audited Facebook, LinkedIn and the website journey.
  • Defined audiences across contractors, developers, architects, engineers, property owners, government and NGOs.
  • Planned educational content, project showcases, testimonials and leadership commentary.
  • Introduced SEO targeting, WhatsApp conversion, paid lead generation and website improvements.

Tools and methods

SEO, AEO, website optimization, LinkedIn strategy, Meta Ads and lead-funnel design

Outcome, evidence and limitations

The project reports that Wonderbuild appeared in a Google AI Overview, providing an early visibility signal. The broader enquiry and order figures are targets for a three-month programme, not confirmed results.
