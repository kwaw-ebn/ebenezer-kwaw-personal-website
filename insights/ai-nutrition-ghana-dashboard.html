A UI/UX case study for automating questionnaire analysis and presenting nutrition-behavior and AI-adoption patterns.

Project typeUI/UX concept with prototype architecture
DisciplineUI/UX and Nutrition Analytics
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

Manual questionnaire processing is time-consuming and may delay interpretation of demographic and nutrition-behavior patterns.

The strategy

Design an upload-to-insight workflow that makes distributions, cross-tabulations and correlations accessible to researchers and nutrition professionals.

My contribution and execution

  • Mapped the workflow from upload through processing, analysis and visualization.
  • Designed demographic, AI-usage and correlation views.
  • Included exportable reports and cross-tabulation concepts.
  • Proposed a full-stack architecture and future extensions for prediction, scheduled imports, multilingual support and field collection.

Tools and methods

UI/UX design, React concept, Node.js concept, Python analytics, PostgreSQL concept, D3.js and Chart.js

Outcome, evidence and limitations

The case study uses AI-generated example data that must be replaced or verified before analysis. It is a UI/UX and product concept, not evidence of a validated nutrition surveillance platform.
