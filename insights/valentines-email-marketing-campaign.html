Valentine’s Email Marketing Campaign

By Ebenezer Kwaw · 25 September 2026 · Campaign concept
View original report ↗View portfolio ↗

A seasonal email campaign concept designed to turn gifting intent into a simple mobile-first purchase journey.

Project typeCampaign concept
DisciplineEmail Marketing
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

Brands compete heavily for attention during Valentine’s season while customers want meaningful and convenient gift options.

The strategy

Combine emotional relevance, a clear offer, urgency and one direct call to action using the AIDA framework.

My contribution and execution

  • Defined audiences including long-distance couples, diaspora gift senders and busy professionals.
  • Developed the campaign idea, offer structure, message hierarchy and mobile-friendly design direction.
  • Mapped the journey from email to product page, purchase and delivery.
  • Defined open-rate, click-through and conversion targets for future measurement.

Tools and methods

Email strategy, AIDA copywriting, customer journey mapping, offer design and mobile-first layout

Outcome, evidence and limitations

This is a campaign concept and execution blueprint. The percentage ranges in the report are expected benchmarks, not achieved campaign results.
