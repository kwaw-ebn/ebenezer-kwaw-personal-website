A digital audit and growth strategy for a menstrual-health, sustainability and women’s-empowerment organization.

Project typeStrategy and audit case study
DisciplineDigital Marketing and Growth
Primary evidenceOriginal project report retained on Google Drive

The challenge

The review identified opportunities around website performance, user experience, brand consistency, SEO visibility, digital storytelling, social proof, partnership presentation and conversion pathways.

The strategy

Build one connected digital ecosystem around website optimization, content marketing, social storytelling, founder visibility, community engagement and partnership conversion.

My contribution and execution

  • Reviewed the website, Instagram, Facebook, blog and partnership presentation.
  • Defined audiences across young women and girls, schools, health institutions, NGOs, donors, CSR partners and sustainability advocates.
  • Developed content pillars for education, impact stories, product communication, founder and team content, and advocacy.
  • Produced website-redesign, SEO, content-calendar and partnership recommendations.

Tools and methods

Digital audit, audience analysis, competitor review, SEO research, content strategy and conversion planning

Outcome, evidence and limitations

The deliverable is a strategic transformation plan and implementation roadmap. It should be read as a proposed growth programme, not evidence that every recommendation has already been implemented or measured.
