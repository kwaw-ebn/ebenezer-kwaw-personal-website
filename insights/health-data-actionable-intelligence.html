Health Data

Moving from data collection to actionable intelligence

By Ebenezer Kwaw · 5 min read

Health teams often collect large amounts of information, yet still struggle to answer time-sensitive programme questions. The gap is rarely a lack of data alone. It is the path between collection, validation, interpretation and action.

Actionable intelligence requires a small set of decision-focused indicators, clear responsibilities, timely review and visual tools that make unusual patterns easier to investigate. Context must travel with the numbers.

The goal is not another dashboard. It is a learning system in which evidence reaches the right person early enough to change what happens next.

Useful knowledge creates value when it reaches the right people early enough to improve a decision.
