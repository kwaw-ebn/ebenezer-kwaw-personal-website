My story

Knowledge matters most when it improves lives.

“How can we use knowledge, data and technology to improve people’s lives?”

My professional journey began in community nutrition and frontline healthcare, where the meaning of data is never abstract: it is connected to people, families and the quality of decisions made every day.

Working within Ghana’s public health system expanded that perspective. My responsibilities have included nutrition programme coordination, monitoring, supportive supervision, stakeholder engagement, health reporting and community education, alongside work supporting HIV services, maternal and child health, immunisation, malaria interventions, wellness programmes and emergency preparedness.

Over time, I became increasingly interested in the systems behind those decisions. That led me into data analytics, machine learning, cloud technology, website development and digital strategy not as separate interests, but as complementary tools for solving real implementation problems.

Professional philosophyTechnology creates its greatest value when it is grounded in human needs, reliable evidence and the realities of implementation.
